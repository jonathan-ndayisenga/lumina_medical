The problem we fix

Your departments have systems. Do they talk to each other?

Most facilities run reception, lab, pharmacy and billing on separate tools or on paper. Patient information gets lost in the handoffs.

Before Ternah

  • Files re-registered at every department, every visit
  • Lab results walked across the compound on paper
  • Services given but never billed
  • Month-end reports put together by hand, days late

With Ternah

  • One patient record shared by every department
  • Results reach the doctor the moment they are ready
  • Every service captured, receipted and reconciled
  • Income and spending reports, always current

FAQ

Questions hospitals ask us.

Do we have to pay for every module?

No. You subscribe only to the departments your facility runs, and you can add more later without changing how you work.

Do we need to install anything?

No. Ternah runs in a web browser, so your staff can use the computers, tablets and phones you already have.

Can it print on our receipt printer?

Yes. Receipts print on 58mm or 80mm thermal paper, or on A4, under your hospital's name.

Is our data kept separate from other hospitals?

Yes. Each hospital's patients, reports and money are kept separate, and every staff member sees only what their role allows.

How do we get help?

Message us on WhatsApp. We are based in Kampala and support facilities hands on.
